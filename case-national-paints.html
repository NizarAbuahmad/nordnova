The role

Head of Marketing for the MENA region, 2022–2024, at a paints manufacturer selling across the Gulf and export markets.

What I inherited

A fragmented marketing setup with no coherent team, strategy or agency structure behind it.

What I built

  • A marketing team of six, hired and led, with the strategy, agency roster and processes built alongside it rather than after
  • The first full brand refresh the company had run in years, taken from brief through to rollout
  • A formal NCS colour-system licensing partnership, aimed at major regional developers
  • A regional events programme running at more than twenty events a year

Why it mattered

Rebuilding a function is not the same as running one. Team, strategy, agencies and process had to come together at once, because fixing any one of them alone would not have held.
