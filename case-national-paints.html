National Paints

Leading a corporate rebrand end to end — agency brief, management approval, legal registration, and rollout across every market the group sells in.

The role

Regional marketing leadership at National Paints, a paints manufacturer selling across the UAE and export markets.

The brief

Rebuild the corporate identity and get it live and consistent across every entity and market the group operates in — not just designed, but adopted.

What I did

  • Led the rebrand with an external creative agency, from the initial brief through to the finished design
  • Took the new identity through management approval, then completed its legal registration
  • Supervised implementation across every touchpoint — catalogues, signboards, the website and social channels
  • Managed the official launch in the UAE, followed by rollout across international markets

Why it mattered

A rebrand only counts once it reaches the market. Getting the design right is the smaller half — approval, registration and consistent rollout across entities is what turns a new identity into something customers actually see.
