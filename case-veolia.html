The company

Veolia Water Technologies, an environmental services group specialising in water treatment solutions.

The role

Marketing communications manager for the region, 2014–2021, leading a team of four across a ten-country footprint.

What I did

  • Rolled out Veolia’s global rebrand across ten countries, in both Arabic and English
  • Took it through every physical and digital surface — signage, uniforms, vehicles, print and digital
  • Launched a new regional website
  • Ran the regional NPS programme, so customer sentiment was measured rather than assumed

Why it mattered

A global rebrand is easy to announce and hard to land. Doing it bilingually across ten markets, down to the vehicles and the uniforms, is where a brand either becomes real or stays a slide.
