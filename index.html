The gap I fill

Most companies don't need another agency. They need someone to run marketing.

Agencies execute. Freelancers do one task. Junior hires need direction. What's missing is the senior person who can think strategically and get into the weeds — someone who's actually owned marketing outcomes, not just advised on them.

That's what I do. Direct access, no account managers, no handoffs to teams you've never met.

When a project needs more hands — design, dev, paid media, production — I bring in trusted specialists I've worked with for years and manage them for you. You get senior thinking and full-team output, without agency overhead or juniors learning on your budget.
