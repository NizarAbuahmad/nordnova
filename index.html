Is this for you?

I work with founders and small-to-mid-sized companies — the size where one senior person can genuinely change the outcome. Being straight about the fit saves us both a call.

A good fit if

  • You’re a founder or an SME with no marketing leader, and marketing keeps landing on someone who already has another job
  • Marketing is happening, but nobody owns whether it works
  • You have a team or an agency and they need senior direction, not more hands
  • You’re launching something, or entering a new GCC or MENA market

Not a fit if

  • You already have a marketing director and a functioning team
  • You want one task executed cheaply — a freelancer will serve you better
  • You want to hand marketing over and never think about it again
