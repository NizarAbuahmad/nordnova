Meta Ads restructure and digital ownership for a dual-curriculum private K–12 school with under-filled KG seats.

Client

First Academy School (FAS), a private K–12 school in West Amman, Jordan, offering dual curriculum (Jordanian national + IGCSE), ~690 students.

Challenge

Low KG enrollment (61/120 seats, ~51% occupancy), inefficient ad spend, and unclear which messaging actually drove enrollment inquiries.

Role

Digital Marketing Manager — full ownership of Meta Ads strategy, execution, and broader digital presence (website, GMB, social).

What I did

  • Restructured the Meta Ads account using ABO (ad set budget) to isolate winning ad sets instead of letting Meta's CBO merge strong and weak performers
  • All-inclusive pricing transparency (tuition + books + uniform + insurance bundled) was the single strongest conversion hook — outperformed generic institutional messaging by 2–3x
  • Shifted all creative to colloquial Jordanian Arabic with emotional parent-focused hooks, dropping formal MSA feature-list copy that underperformed
  • Cleaned up account hygiene: excluded Audience Network (the main budget leak), removed view-through attribution errors, disabled Advantage+ auto-enhancements that were overriding Arabic copy
  • Built a systematic budget-scaling discipline (max 20–30% increases every 3–4 days) instead of reactive spend changes
  • Diagnosed retargeting fatigue (frequency ~4.28) and refreshed with real classroom video creative
  • Grounded upcoming creative direction in actual parent satisfaction survey data (n=45)

What changed

Kindergarten went from just over half full to every seat taken, and pricing-led creative in colloquial Jordanian Arabic cut the cost of an enrolment enquiry to roughly a third of what formal, feature-list copy had been costing.
