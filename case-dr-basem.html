Digital presence build · Syrian market

Dr. Basem Al-Aboud

Brand authority and a digital presence built from zero for a Syrian urologist, ahead of a Damascus clinic launch.

drbasem-urology.com ↗

Client

Dr. Basem Al-Aboud (@dr.basem.urology) — Syrian urologist and oncology specialist based in Böblingen, Germany, planning to open a medical center in Damascus within an 18-month horizon.

Challenge

Build brand authority and a digital presence from zero, targeting Syrian Arabic-speaking patients — specifically Damascus, rural Damascus, and the Eastern Region (Deir ez-Zor, Raqqa, Hasakah) — ahead of a physical clinic launch. Key constraint: target audience (Syrian men) consumes health content privately due to stigma around urology/men's health topics, making public engagement metrics unreliable signals of success.

Strategy

  • Positioned Dr. Basem on a dual-identity hook: European medical credentials + authentic Syrian cultural voice — differentiated from competitors' more commercial tone
  • Sensitivity-sequenced content: safe physical topics (kidney stones, BPH, blood in urine) published first to build trust before introducing more sensitive topics (ED, fertility, marital health)
  • First-person, Syrian Levantine dialect captions written as if Dr. Basem is speaking directly
  • Native platform uploads (Facebook, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) — no cross-posting, to avoid algorithmic deprioritization
  • Paid Meta campaigns geo-targeted 100% to Syria, with a three-phase strategy (warm audience → cold Eastern Region expansion → retargeting) built around a Damascus visit campaign
  • Competitive benchmarking against Dr. Yaman Al-Tal (6M+ followers) — adapting his hook/myth-vs-fact structure without copying
  • WhatsApp-first patient intake, backed by an Arabic Google Form/Sheets qualification system

Results (under 2 months)

  • Facebook: 30,000 followers
  • Instagram: 24,000 followers
  • Campaigns confirmed 100% delivery to Syria with zero geographic leakage
  • Frequent urination content outperformed kidney stones content by ~67% more clicks at equivalent spend, informing budget reallocation

Key learning

High view counts alone aren't the success metric for this audience — DMs, watch time, return viewers, and WhatsApp conversations are treated as the real indicators, since public likes/comments/shares understate true engagement given cultural stigma.
