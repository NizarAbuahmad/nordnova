Afro Baobab Cultural Hub

Positioning and go-to-market for a permanent immersive African cultural destination in Al Quoz, ahead of its opening.

Afro Baobab Cultural Hub

The client

Afro Baobab Cultural Hub is a permanent immersive African cultural destination opening in Al Quoz, Dubai — founded by Saran Sow Barry after years of hosting sold-out immersive African experiences in her own home. What began as storytelling, cuisine, and music nights evolved into a purpose-built cultural institution: rotating exhibitions, workshops, performances, and experiential learning under one roof.

The challenge

Position an unprecedented category — not a museum, not an events venue, not an activity center — in a hypercompetitive, multicultural market, while building the commercial engine (schools, corporates, public programming) needed to sustain it long-term, all ahead of a physical space still in fitout.

My role

Brand & marketing consultant, working directly with the founder and design/dev lead to take the concept from positioning through go-to-market.

What I built

  • Brand positioning & verbal identity — "Experience. Connect. Belong." Rooted in Ubuntu and Teranga philosophy, Africa-led with inclusive framing, translated into a full messaging and content system
  • Commercial architecture — three-pillar revenue model (schools, corporates, public programming) with a 6-pipeline CRM built in Zoho and a 55-email automation library
  • Launch campaign — the Founding Creators Collection, a paid workshop series used as the first commercial proof point, with a full Meta Ads structure (WhatsApp lead gen + landing page traffic) built for a fragmented, small-budget account
  • Content & editorial system — real-photography-only brand rule, phased September launch plan (Convert → Prove → Open the Doors), and a governed content calendar
  • Website & legal foundation — live Webflow site with UAE PDPL-compliant policies

Principles I applied

Real photography only — no AI-generated people in paid media. Every date and price gated through founder sign-off. "Authorship not sponsorship" for all partnerships. Schools always positioned first as the anchor revenue pillar.

Status

In-flight: fitout underway, founding workshops launching September 2026, CRM and pixel infrastructure being finalized ahead of the public opening.
