What an audit actually looks like

Every audit I deliver follows the same spine. No client is named here — this is the structure, not anyone’s data.

Current situationAn honest read of where marketing stands today — what exists, what doesn’t, and what is quietly costing money.
Focus areasWhere effort should concentrate next, and just as importantly, what to stop doing.
Team & structureWhat the marketing function needs to look like to deliver the plan — roles, ownership, and what to outsource.
TimelinePhased actions with sequence and owners, so the report becomes a plan rather than a critique.
KPIsWhat you measure from here, per channel, so progress is arguable with numbers rather than opinions.
